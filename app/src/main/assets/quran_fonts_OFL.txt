Why these two
-------------
The reading-options sheet offers five Quran fonts. Only faces whose licence
permits redistribution inside an Apache-2.0 application are bundled. The
SIL OFL grants exactly that: the fonts may be used, studied, modified and
redistributed, including commercially, provided they are not sold on their
own and any derivative keeps the licence.

The other three faces offered in the picker (KFGQ, MeQuran and Naskh
Nastaleeq / Noorani Quran) are NOT bundled, because their own licences do not
allow it:

  KFGQPC Uthmanic Script HAFS
      "All rights reserved. This Font is the property of King Fahd Glorious
       Quran Printing Complex, and may not be reproduced..." (EULA, (c) 2010)

  me_quran
      "Typeface and data (c) 2005, Meor Ridzuan. Free for non commercial use."

  AlQuran IndoPak / Naskh Nastaleeq
      Custom end-user terms, (c) Ayman Siddiqui / QuranWBW.com, all rights
      reserved.

Relicensing this application does not change any of the above: those terms are
granted by the font's own copyright holder, not by this project's licence.

Adding one of them later
------------------------
QuranFonts in ui/theme/QuranFonts.kt resolves each slot by resource name and
falls back cleanly when the file is absent, so enabling a face you have
permission for is a two-step change:

  1. Drop the .ttf / .otf into this directory, e.g. quran_kfgqpc.otf
  2. Point the matching QuranFontFace entry at it:

         val fontRes = R.font.quran_kfgqpc

No other file needs to change, and the picker entry starts working.
