Why these five
--------------
The reading-options sheet offers a Quranic typeface. The picker used to list seven
and ship two: KFGQ, MeQuran, Digital Khatt, Naskh Nastaleeq and Noorani were all
offered, all resolved to a fallback, and all drew the same face. A control that
looks like a choice and is not one is worse than no control, so the list is now
exactly the faces this app can render.

The OFL grants precisely what an Apache-2.0 application needs: the fonts may be
used, studied, modified and redistributed, including commercially, provided they
are not sold on their own and any derivative keeps the licence.

Every one of the five was also checked for **U+06DD**, ARABIC END OF AYAH, before
being added. That codepoint is a standalone ornament rather than a numeric
placeholder, and a mushaf whose ayah markers are tofu boxes is not a mushaf.
Reem Kufi - also SIL OFL - was considered and rejected on exactly that: a
beautiful face that cannot draw the mark.

Adding a face later
-------------------
Drop the .ttf / .otf into app/src/main/res/font/ with a lowercase name, add a
QuranFontFace entry pointing at it with a line-height factor suited to its script,
and put its licence in this directory. That is the whole change - and the
invariant the tests check is that every face the picker offers can be drawn, so a
face without a file cannot be added by accident.
